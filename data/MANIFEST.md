## Known gaps

* 12 minor species have no 2019 mixing ratio in the AR6 file and 4 (c-C4F8, i-C6F14, C7F16, C8F18)
  have no radiative efficiency in the metrics table; they are skipped and reported by
  `halogen_forcing(...)["skipped"]`. Their combined AR6 ERF in 2019 is about 0.002 W m-2.
* Licence of the AR6 numbers themselves (IPCC) allows reuse with citation; confirm before
  redistributing the raw files in a packaged release.

## Non-GHG forcing emulators (added with `radforce.forcing.nonghg`)

* Aerosol-radiation and stratospheric water vapour reproduce the AR6 series essentially exactly (<1e-6 W m-2).
* Aerosol-cloud: the two shape factors in `constants.py` are a **least-squares fit to the AR6 median
  ERFaci series** (max error 0.008 W m-2), because the 12 CMIP6 model fits disagree by orders of magnitude
  and the AR6 ensemble samples are not public. They are an emulator parameterisation, not a measurement.
* Ozone: the AR6 authors' own precursor fit; max difference from the AR6 series 0.076 W m-2 (about 1970).
* Black carbon on snow: max difference 0.013 W m-2 (the AR6 series used CEDS BC without biomass burning).
* Land use and contrails are simple multipliers on the AR6 2019 value; the aviation-NOx and land-use-flux
  inputs behind the AR6 series were not available in the public repository tree.
* Every emulator is normalised so that 2019 drivers give exactly the AR6 2019 value and 1750 drivers give zero.

Atmosphere: U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976 (NOAA/NASA/USAF); water vapour from the fixed relative humidity

### Known gaps and decisions
* CFC/HFC/SF6/PFC cross-sections are in the Spectra tab (see below).
* **CF4 is excluded**: its HITRAN line list peaks near 1255 cm-1 while the true nu3 maximum is at 1283 cm-1.
* No CO2 line mixing; one US-standard profile; clouds only as the calibrated global-mean estimate: see the limitations on the Spectra tab.

## HITRAN infrared cross-section files (`data/raw/hitran_xsec/<MOLECULE>/*.xsc`, git-ignored)

Downloaded manually from <https://hitran.org/xsc/> (free account) on 2026-11-07; each folder keeps the HITRAN
`.bib` citation file.  **17 molecules, 648 `.xsc` files, about 1.3 GB**, covering **97.1 %** of the 2019 AR6
halogenated-gas forcing (0.396 of 0.408 W m-2).  Largest species not downloaded: CFC-115 (0.0021), Halon-1301,
Halon-1211, CH2Cl2, CFC-13 (about 0.001 each).

| Molecule | Main data set (used first) | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| CFC-11, CFC-12, HCFC-22, HCFC-141b | air-broadened (T, p) grids, 30-32 files, 188-295 K, 7.5-760 Torr | good |
| HFC-23, -32, -125, -134a | same kind of grid, 27-30 files | good |
| SF6 (780-1100), CF4 (1190-1336), C2F6 (1061-1165, 1220-1285), CCl4 (700-860) | (T, p) grids, 26-43 files | good (C2F6 and CCl4 cover only their strongest bands) |
| CFC-113, CFC-114, HCFC-142b, HFC-143a, C3F8 | older measurements, mostly temperature-only (pressure not recorded, treated as ~1 atm), plus the N2-broadened 760 Torr sets at 278/298/323 K | adequate for broad, unresolved bands; no pressure dependence |

* Most molecules also have wide-range N2-broadened files (about 500-6500 cm-1, 760 Torr, 278-323 K) used to fill gaps
  outside the main grid.
* **Cleaned up on 2026-11-07:** removed all `.bib.html` files; moved the folders from `data/raw/hitran/` (HAPI line-list
  database) to `data/raw/hitran_xsec/`; deleted three superseded duplicate sets: CFC-11 narrow windows
  810-880 / 1050-1120 (110 files), CF4 1250-1290 high-resolution (55 files), SF6 925-955 (29 files).  They can be
  re-downloaded from HITRAN if ever needed.  Other older or overlapping sets were kept as cross-checks.
* **One corrupted value** exists in `C2F6_284.1_520.5_1061.0-1165.0_02.xsc` (token `6341E-21`); the loader replaces it by
  zero and warns.  All files have the usual last-line zero padding, which the loader drops.
* **Completeness check** (`build/halocarbon_check.py`, results in `docs/halocarbon_check.md`): computed radiative
  efficiencies are a nearly constant 1.7-2.0 times AR6's, with no outlier, so no data set is missing a band; the
  common factor is the cloud/continuum effect on window absorbers, which a simple opaque-deck cloud model reduces to
  0.83-1.10 of AR6.

## Water-vapour continuum: MT_CKD 4.3 (`data/raw/mtckd/`)

| File | Content | Source | Licence | Retrieved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `absco-ref_wv-mt-ckd.nc` | MT_CKD 4.3 self and foreign water-vapour continuum reference coefficients (296 K, 1013 mbar) and self temperature exponent | AER-RC/MT_CKD_H2O (github.com/AER-RC/MT_CKD_H2O), Mlawer et al. (2012) Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 370, 2520 | AER licence (`LICENSE.md` alongside): free for scientific and research use; redistribution allowed with the AER copyright notice reproduced and acknowledgement; **not to be incorporated into commercial software**. Acknowledgement: Atmospheric and Environmental Research, Inc. (AER) | 2026-11-07 |
| `mt_ckd_h2o_user_guide.pdf`, `LICENSE.md` | documentation and licence | same | same | 2026-11-07 |

Implemented in `radforce.spectra.continuum` exactly as in the reference Fortran (`mt_ckd_h2o_module.f90`): density and temperature
scaling, radiation term nu tanh(h c nu / 2kT), cubic interpolation in wavenumber.  The standard `for_absco_ref` foreign continuum is used (not the
alternative `for_closure_absco_ref`).  Because MT_CKD is defined relative to water-vapour lines cut 25 cm-1 from line centre, the H2O lines use that
cut-off.  Effect on the model: clear-sky OLR 275.2 W m-2 (3.5 cm-1 wings, no continuum) to 264.0 W m-2 (observed 268.1).

## Observational constraint used for the all-sky estimate

Global-mean longwave cloud radiative effect 27.9 W m-2 (all-sky OLR 240.1, clear-sky OLR 268.1), CERES EBAF Ed4.0, July 2005 - June 2015, with net-flux
constraint: Loeb, N. G. et al. (2018) *Clouds and the Earth's Radiant Energy System (CERES) Energy Balanced and Filled (EBAF) Top-of-Atmosphere
(TOA) Edition-4.0 Data Product*, J. Climate 31, 895-918, doi:10.1175/JCLI-D-17-0208.1, Table 5 (read from the paper on 2026-11-07).
Global-mean cloud fraction 0.67 is a round satellite-climatology value, not read from a specific table.

## Emission metrics (`build/build_metrics.py`, `radforce/metrics.py`)

Inputs are the AR6 Table 7.SM.7 species list already in `raw/ar6/metrics_supplement_cleaned.csv` (lifetime, radiative efficiency, AR6 metrics).
Molar masses are derived from the formulae. The impulse-response and temperature-response constants (Joos et al. 2013; Gasser et al. 2017; the AR6
two-timescale response) are written out, with sources, in `radforce/metrics.py`. Output: `web/public/data/metrics.json`.

## Stratospheric adjustment (`build/stratospheric_adjustment.py`, `radforce/spectra/adjust.py`)

Computed from the same cached cross-sections and atmosphere as the Spectra tab; no new data. Output: `web/public/data/adjustment.json`.
